# Mod Settings 0.1.0

## New
- A side panel in the desktop app with every installed mod's settings, one card per mod. Open it with `/doole_settings`.
- Change settings in place: On/Off buttons, drop-downs for choices, and fields with Save for text and numbers. A line under the header says what changed or why it was refused.
- In the terminal it points you to `/config`.
- Long names and values are cut with "…" so they stay inside the panel.
